The app aims to create a smart alarm clock.
The user will create an alarm (behaves like a normal alarm clock there), while the alarm may only be turned off by scanning different items (or codes on stickers) or perform specific actions (physical and intellectual).
All items and actions are chosen by the user. Specific approach (predefined set of items/actions, fully customisable set) may be chosen at a later stage.
Object scanning will be achieved using a local VLM (specifics TBA).
An alarm may always have a bypass method, the last available way to turn it off being inserting a password (with specific standards).
The project aims for maximum device compatibility on mobile (smartphone) devices.
